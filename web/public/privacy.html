Roomprint

Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Roomprint turns a scan of your rooms into a floor plan, a 3D model and a walkthrough video. It is a small test project run by one person, not a company.

What gets uploaded

  • The scan: wall, door, window and furniture positions measured by the iPhone (a free scan has none of these).
  • A video of the walk, with sound from the microphone.
  • The depth measurements and small camera photos taken during the scan, used to build the coloured 3D model.
  • Where the camera was and which way it pointed for each frame of the video, inside the scanned space (not a location on a map), used to paint the 3D model from the video.
  • The space's name, which the app makes from the date.
  • A random number the app makes up for itself when it is installed, and the phone's model (such as "iPhone 15 Pro"), so the person running Roomprint can tell which spaces came from the same phone. It says nothing else about you or the phone, is not shown to people who open a space's link, and is not used anywhere outside Roomprint.

Nothing else: no account, no name, no location, no contacts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking across apps or sites. The app uses the camera and microphone only while you are scanning, and says so on screen.

Where it is kept

On a private server run by the developer in the European Union, until you delete it. It is not sold or shared with anyone and not used to train AI models. Floor plans, 3D models, the photo-like "splat" view you can ask for, and the files you export are all made on that server, from your scan alone, and are deleted with it. There are no backup copies.

Who can see it

Every space has its own link that cannot be guessed. Anyone who has the link can see the space, including the video, and download its files and exports, so only share it with people you trust. The developer can see all spaces, to keep the service working.

Deleting your data

In the app, open a space and its menu: under Files on the server swipe a file to delete it, or tap Delete this space. You can also swipe a space on the start screen. Deleting removes the files from the server right away and for good: there is no trash and no backup, and the link stops working for everyone. Uploads still waiting on the phone are cancelled.

Only the phone that made a space can delete it. If you uploaded through a link someone sent you, or lost your phone, write to {{CONTACT}} with the space's link and it will be deleted, usually the same day. Spaces may also be deleted when the test ends.
