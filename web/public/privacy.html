Roomprint

Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Roomprint turns a scan of your rooms into a floor plan, a 3D model and a walkthrough video. It is a small test project run by one person, not a company.

What gets uploaded

  • The room scan: wall, door, window and furniture positions measured by the iPhone.
  • A video of the walk, with sound from the microphone.
  • The depth measurements and small camera photos taken during the scan, used to build the coloured 3D model.
  • The space and room names you type.

Nothing else: no account, no name, no location, no contacts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app uses the camera and microphone only while you are scanning, and says so on screen.

Where it is kept

On a private server run by the developer in the European Union, until you delete it. It is not sold or shared with anyone and not used to train AI models. Floor plans and 3D models are made on that server. There are no backup copies.

Who can see it

Every space has its own link that cannot be guessed. Anyone who has the link can see the space, including the video, and download its files, so only share it with people you trust. The developer can see all spaces, to keep the service working.

Deleting your data

In the app, open a space: swipe a file to delete it, or tap Delete this space from the server. You can also swipe a space on the start screen. Deleting removes the files from the server right away and for good: there is no trash and no backup, and the link stops working for everyone. Uploads still waiting on the phone are cancelled.

Only the phone that made a space can delete it. If you uploaded through a link someone sent you, or lost your phone, write to {{CONTACT}} with the space's link and it will be deleted, usually the same day. Spaces may also be deleted when the test ends.
