Roomprint

Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Roomprint turns a scan of your rooms into a floor plan, a 3D model and a walkthrough video. It is a small test project run by one person, not a company.

What the app uploads

  • The room scan: wall, door, window and furniture positions measured by the iPhone.
  • A video of the walk, with sound from the microphone.
  • The depth measurements and small camera pictures taken during the scan, used to build the 3D model.
  • The space and room names you type.

Nothing else: no account, no name, no location, no contacts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app uses the camera and microphone only while you are scanning.

Where it is kept

On a private server run by the developer in the European Union. It is not sold or shared with anyone, and it is not used to train AI models. A space's floor plan, 3D model, video and files are processed on that server only.

Who can see it

Every space has its own link that cannot be guessed. Anyone who has the link can see the space, including the video, so only share it with people you trust. The developer can see all spaces to keep the service working.

Deleting your data

Write to {{CONTACT}} with the space's link and it will be deleted, usually the same day. Spaces may also be removed when the test ends.
