How to film

  • Best: one continuous walk through all rooms, without stopping the recording.
  • Turn the lights on and open the curtains.
  • Walk slowly along the walls, phone at chest height, camera pointing into the room.
  • Film every corner, and pan a little up to the ceiling line and down to the floor.
  • When going from room to room, film through the doorway as you walk through.
  • Landscape or portrait both work. Keep moving slowly; no fast turns.
  • Record with the Camera app, then upload from your library: filming straight from this page ("Take Video") gives a much smaller, blurrier video.
  • Have the Roomprint app on a LiDAR iPhone (Pro models)? Scan in the app instead: it measures every wall to the centimetre.
