Roomprint

Walk through your home with an iPhone. Get a measured floor plan, a 3D model and a video of every room.

How it works

  1. Scan. Open the app and walk slowly through each room. The iPhone's LiDAR sensor measures every wall, door and window while the camera films the walk, with sound, so you can say each room's name as you go.
  2. Upload. When you finish, the scan and the video go to the Roomprint server. The floor plan is ready a few seconds later.
  3. Look and share. Every space gets a private link with four views: a 3D model you can turn around, a printable blueprint with wall lengths, the walkthrough video and the uploaded files.

Get the app (TestFlight)

Roomprint is not in the App Store yet. It is shared through TestFlight, Apple's own app for trying apps before they are released. It is free, and it's safe: the app comes from Apple's servers, like any App Store app.

  1. Ask for an invite with the form below. Give the email address of the Apple ID on your iPhone.
  2. Install TestFlight from the App Store: TestFlight on the App Store.
  3. Open the invite email on your iPhone and tap View in TestFlight. That opens TestFlight with your invite code already filled in.
  4. Tap Install. Roomprint appears on your home screen. New versions show up in TestFlight, and it can update them for you.
Scanning needs an iPhone with LiDAR: the Pro and Pro Max models from iPhone 12 Pro on. Other iPhones can install the app and open shared plans, but cannot scan. To report a problem, take a screenshot in the app: TestFlight offers to send it to us with a note.
